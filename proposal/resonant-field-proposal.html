Design presentation

Resonant Field design proposal

ResonantOS brings conversation, inspectable sources, drafts, and human decisions into one workspace. Its material system should make those relationships tangible and let the person choose the atmosphere.

This presentation explains the design direction, material studies, evidence and remaining decisions. The app prototype is a separate interactive view, with Workspace and Augmentor surfaces. Generated imagery is used only for optional background plates.

  1. 01
    Personal atmosphere

    Choose a grayscale plate and a material palette independently. Nature, architecture, texture, and plain backgrounds belong to the same system.

  2. 02
    Purposeful layers

    Five material levels belong to components and states: recessed reading, shared work panes, raised decisions, and floating review controls. Column order does not set depth.

  3. 03
    Explicit trust

    Source provenance and permission use words and structure. Light and color support those labels.

  4. 04
    One system, two surfaces

    The workspace and Resonant Field Augmentor proposal share appearance values. The Original source comparison preserves its own skin.

Material behavior

Color lives in the panes.

The setting is grayscale by default, with an optional single complementary tint. Five related films vary around the chosen main hue. Their color and opacity accumulate, while each added level contributes to the blur budget. Recessed surfaces expose an earlier material path; text and controls stay sharp.

Components use the path appropriate to their purpose and state. The review action occupies the fifth level at rest and settles one level when pressed. Visual harmony and mode parity still require acceptance; the model is an authored relationship, not a physical optical simulation.
